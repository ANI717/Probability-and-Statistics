# Individuals and Variables

## Individual

An **individual** is an object, person, animal, event, or other entity being described by a dataset.

Example:

| Student | Age | Major |
|---|---:|---|
| Alice | 20 | Biology |
| Bob | 22 | Computer Science |

The individuals are **Alice and Bob**.

## Variable

A **variable** is a characteristic measured or recorded for each individual.

In the example:

- `Age` is a variable.
- `Major` is a variable.

Think of a dataset as:

- **Rows → individuals**
- **Columns → variables**

## Example

| Car | Color | MPG | Transmission |
|---|---|---:|---|
| A | Red | 28 | Automatic |
| B | Blue | 32 | Manual |

Individuals:
- Car A
- Car B

Variables:
- Color
- MPG
- Transmission

## Key Idea

**Individual = who/what is being studied**

**Variable = what is being recorded about them**
