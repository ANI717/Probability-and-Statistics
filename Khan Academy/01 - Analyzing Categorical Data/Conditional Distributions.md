# Conditional Distributions

A **conditional distribution** describes the distribution of one variable **within a particular group** of another variable.

Consider:

| | Coffee | No Coffee | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Men | 30 | 20 | 50 |
| Women | 40 | 10 | 50 |
| Total | 70 | 30 | 100 |

## Coffee Preference Among Men

We only consider men.

Total men = 50

Coffee:

30 / 50 = 60%

No Coffee:

20 / 50 = 40%

Conditional distribution among men:

- Coffee → 60%
- No Coffee → 40%

## Coffee Preference Among Women

Total women = 50

Coffee:

40 / 50 = 80%

No Coffee:

10 / 50 = 20%

Conditional distribution among women:

- Coffee → 80%
- No Coffee → 20%

## Key Idea

The denominator is the size of the group being conditioned on.

"Among men" means:

Denominator = Total Men

"Among women" means:

Denominator = Total Women

## Conditional vs Marginal

Marginal:

70 / 100 = 70% of **everyone** likes coffee.

Conditional:

30 / 50 = 60% of **men** like coffee.

## Finding Relationships

Conditional distributions are especially useful for determining whether categorical variables are associated.

If conditional distributions differ substantially between groups, an association may exist.
