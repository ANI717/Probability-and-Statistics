# Frequency and Relative Frequency

## Frequency

**Frequency** is the number of times a category occurs.

Example:

| Color | Frequency |
|---|---:|
| Red | 20 |
| Blue | 30 |
| Green | 50 |

Total:

20 + 30 + 50 = 100

## Relative Frequency

**Relative frequency** is the proportion of observations belonging to a category.

Formula:

Relative Frequency = Category Frequency / Total Frequency

Example:

Blue:

30 / 100 = 0.30 = 30%

## Difference

Frequency answers:

> How many?

Relative frequency answers:

> What proportion or percentage?

## Example

If 40 out of 200 students prefer online classes:

Frequency:

40

Relative frequency:

40 / 200 = 0.20 = 20%

## Important

Relative frequencies across all categories should add to:

1

or

100%

apart from small rounding differences.
